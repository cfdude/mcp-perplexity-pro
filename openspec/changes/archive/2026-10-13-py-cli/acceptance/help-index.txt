# perplexity-pro 2.0.0
Web-grounded answers, search, decisions and embeddings; every costed call is recorded.
Routing: standard, default model perplexity/sonar.

## Pick a tool
- A quick grounded fact: perplexity_ask
- A report that takes minutes: perplexity_research, then perplexity_jobs
- Links and snippets, no answer: perplexity_search
- A follow-up conversation: perplexity_chat
- Classify a text: perplexity_decide

## Tools
- perplexity_ask: billed
- perplexity_chat: billed
- perplexity_decide: billed
- perplexity_embed: billed
- perplexity_help: free
- perplexity_jobs: free
- perplexity_models: free
- perplexity_projects: free
- perplexity_research: billed, async
- perplexity_search: billed
